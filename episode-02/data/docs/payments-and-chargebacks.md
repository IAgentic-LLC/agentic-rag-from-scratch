# Payments and Chargebacks
Owner: Payments team. Last reviewed: 2026-07-01.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Klarna (Klarna in the EU and the UK only). Apple Pay and Google Pay work wherever the card network works.

## Refund timing
We issue the refund within 5 business days of the return being scanned. After that, the customer's bank or PayPal can take up to 10 more business days to show it. Klarna refunds are applied to the customer's Klarna balance on the same day we issue them.

## Chargebacks
If a customer disputes a payment with their bank, do not issue a second refund. Send the order reference to the Payments team within 2 business days; they answer the bank with the evidence. Disputes about drop orders must include the drop-day order log.

## Limits
Single orders above 1,500 EUR (or the equivalent in GBP or USD) are held for manual review for up to 24 hours.
