# Regional Guides
Owner: Logistics. Last reviewed: 2026-08-20.

## EU
Prices include VAT. Parcels are delivered by our EU carriers and need no customs forms. Returns are free with a prepaid label.

## UK
Prices include VAT. Parcels pass customs after Brexit, and Dropline pays the duties (delivered duty paid). Returns are free with a prepaid label.

## US
Prices exclude sales tax, which is added at checkout. Returns are free with a prepaid label at any drop-off point.
