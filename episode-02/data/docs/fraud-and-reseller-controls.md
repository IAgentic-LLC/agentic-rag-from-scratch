# Fraud and Reseller Controls
Owner: CX and Fraud. Last reviewed: 2026-06-15. These controls were introduced after INC-2047.

## Purchase limits
Drops are limited to 1 pair per customer per release. Orders above the limit are cancelled automatically and the customer is told by email.

## Account review
The Fraud team reviews every account with more than 3 drop returns in 90 days. The review takes up to 2 business days, and the account cannot buy in a drop while it is under review.

## Shared addresses
More than 2 accounts that ship to the same address in one drop are flagged for review. Households with several members can ask support to add a household note.

## Escalation
Support agents who suspect reselling must not accuse the customer. Add the tag "fraud-review" and the case goes to the Fraud team lead.
