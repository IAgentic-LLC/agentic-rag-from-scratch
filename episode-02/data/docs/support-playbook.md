# Support Playbook
Owner: Customer Experience. Last reviewed: 2026-08-12.

## Tone
Be short and warm. Use the customer's name once. Never promise a date for a refund; say "within 5 business days of the return being scanned".

## Escalation
Escalate to a team lead when a refund is above 300 EUR (or the equivalent), when a customer asks for a goodwill exception, or when a customer has contacted us 3 times about the same order.

## Goodwill exceptions
Agents can approve one goodwill exception per customer per year together with a team lead. Record the reason in the case notes.

## Macros
Use the macro "Drop window" to explain the 7-day drop rule and the macro "EU rights" to explain the 14-day rule.
