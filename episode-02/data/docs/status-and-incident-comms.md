# Status Page and Incident Communication
Owner: Engineering management. Last reviewed: 2026-05-30.

## Severity levels
SEV-1 and SEV-2 incidents are posted on the public status page. SEV-3 incidents are not.

## SEV-1 updates
Post the first status update within 15 minutes of the page. Then update every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved.

## Postmortems
A postmortem is published internally within 5 business days after a SEV-1 or SEV-2 is resolved. It uses the sections What happened, Impact and Fixes or Actions.

## Escalation
If the status page cannot be updated, ask the head of engineering to approve an email notice to partners.
