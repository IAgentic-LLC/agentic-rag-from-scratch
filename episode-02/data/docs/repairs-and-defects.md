# Repairs and Defects
Owner: Customer Experience. Last reviewed: 2026-05-20.

## Covered defects
Manufacturing defects such as sole separation, broken stitching and faulty eyelets are covered. Normal wear, fading and damage from machine washing are not covered.

## Exceptions
Claims for defects do not need the original box. A photo of the defect and the order number are required.

## Repairs
Covered repairs are done by our partner workshop in Rotterdam and take 10 to 15 business days. Customers pay no shipping for covered repairs. If a repair is not possible, we send a replacement pair or a refund.

## Returns for defects
Defective items can also be returned for a refund within 90 days of delivery, see the Refund and Returns Policy. Later defects are assessed case by case by Customer Experience.
