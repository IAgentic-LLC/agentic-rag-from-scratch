# Holiday Returns and Support Hours
Owner: Customer Experience. Last reviewed: 2026-09-01.

## Holiday returns
Regular items bought between 15 November and 24 December can be returned until 31 January of the next year. This replaces the 30-day window for those orders. Limited drops are not part of the holiday extension and keep their 7-day window (14 days for EU customers).

## Support hours
Support is open Monday to Friday 08:00 to 20:00 CET, and Saturday 10:00 to 16:00 CET. During a limited drop the chat stays open until 22:00 CET.

## Closed days
Support is closed on 25 December, 26 December and 1 January. Urgent SEV-1 issues are handled by the on-call rotation as usual.
