# Refund and Returns Policy (archived)
Owner: Customer Experience. Last reviewed: 2025-09-12. Status: SUPERSEDED on 2026-04-01 by the current Refund and Returns Policy. Kept for reference only; do not apply it to new orders.

## Standard returns
Regular catalogue items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unworn, in the original box, with tags attached. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days of the return being scanned at our warehouse.

## Limited drops
Items bought in a limited drop had the same 30-day return window as regular items.

## EU customers
Customers with an EU delivery address have the legal 14-day right of withdrawal.

## Exceptions
Defective items could be returned within 60 days. Goodwill exceptions needed approval from the head of Customer Experience.
