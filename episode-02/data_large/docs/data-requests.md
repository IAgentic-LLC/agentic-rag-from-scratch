# Customer Data Requests
Owner: Legal and Privacy. Last reviewed: 2026-03-10.

## Access and export
Customers can ask for a copy of their personal data. We reply within 30 days. Support agents forward the request to privacy@dropline.example and must not export data themselves.

## Deletion
A deletion request removes the account, the order history and the saved addresses within 30 days. Records needed for tax law are kept for 7 years but are no longer linked to the account.

## Verification
Before forwarding any request, confirm that it comes from the email address on the account.
