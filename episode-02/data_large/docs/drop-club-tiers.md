# Drop Club Tiers
Owner: Growth. Last reviewed: 2026-08-05.

## Gold tier
Members with 2,000 points or more become Gold. Gold members get a priority support queue and two free express upgrades per month.

## Points expiry
Points expire 12 months after they were earned.

## Benefits
Gold members get the same early access as all members, 10 minutes before the public release. They do not get a bigger allocation: the limit is still 1 pair per customer per release.
